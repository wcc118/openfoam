Verification and provenance

79 tests passed; nine native solves terminated normally. Mesh refinement changed static compliance under 0.7%; first frequency by 0.34%. Higher modes are not fully mesh-converged or classified. This view uses the fine mesh with 96 rotational AUTOSPC constraints; no translational AUTOSPC. Disabling AUTOSPC changed torsion compliance 0.181%. Modal mass-diagonal warnings are documented. Tip-local distortion makes fitted tip twist unsuitable as a uniform GJ estimate.
