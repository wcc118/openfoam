How to read induced crossflow

Induced vectors are (0, Uy − U∞y, Uz − U∞z), with the axial component omitted. Their color magnitude uses those same two components. This exposes motion around the tip; it is not total airspeed. Arrow length is a fixed display scale per mode, not a particle trajectory. No interpolation between angles or unsampled slice locations.
