    When the quick model helps

    Use the UVLM sweep to inspect trends, compare candidate operating points and choose where to investigate. Its incompressible, fixed-wake surrogate does not model separation or viscous boundary layers.

    When more compute earns its cost

    Use CFD to examine effects omitted by the quick model and resolve uncertainties that could change a decision. Here the mesh/settings sensitivity and load imbalance still need resolving before longer coupled dynamics.

    What “accurate” would require

    Numerical checks and agreement between models are useful evidence. Actual-wing accuracy remains unknown here: no modal-test or wind-tunnel correlation is attached.

    Observed compute cost, with its scope

    These are different tasks on different execution paths, not a controlled speedup benchmark. More runtime alone does not establish a better answer.
